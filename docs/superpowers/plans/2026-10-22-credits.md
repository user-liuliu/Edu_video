# Credits Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans.

**Goal:** Ship targeted redemption, account ledger and 10-credit successful video generation billing.
**Architecture:** Row-locked wallets, immutable ledger, hashed targeted codes, unique job reservations. Reserve within submission; settle within worker-success transaction; release on failure. Read available funds from active job holds so terminal/deleted tasks never strand funds.
**Tech Stack:** Vue 3, Pinia, Element Plus, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy/PostgreSQL, Alembic.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-credits-design.md

## Global Constraints
- 10 points per successful video generation, failures/cancellation/export free; no automatic grants.
- Preserve existing changes; isolated worktree and disposable test DB.

## Review Focus
- Concurrency and replay cannot overdraw or duplicate a credit/debit.
- Invalid/expired/wrong-user codes reveal no owner and never credit.
- Worker retries/failed/deleted tasks do not strand holds.
- Ledger survives deletion and retains resulting balance.
- Mobile/collapsed balance stays reachable; load failure is not rendered as zero.
